01
Classifier Agent
Domain Taxonomy & Adaptive Policy Routing
In Simplest Words (ELI5)

Imagine walking into a hospital. The triage nurse doesn't perform surgery immediately—they first check what's wrong with you to send you to the right specialist!

Similarly, the Classifier Agent reads the opening paragraphs of your uploaded document to determine: "What kind of document is this?" Is it a Resume/CV? A Medical Record? A Court Filing? Or a Commercial Contract? Different documents have completely different privacy rules! For instance, in a CV, your previous company name is public, but your personal phone number is private. In a hospital record, a doctor's diagnosis must be masked under HIPAA. The Classifier ensures the right rules are selected from second one.

Under The Hood (Technical Architecture)
  • Fast Window Slicing: Reads the first 1,500 characters of the document where titles, headers, and domain indicators reside.
  • Structured LLM Inference: Queries Google Gemini 3.8 Flash with low temperature (0.1) for deterministic JSON classification.
  • Domain Mapping: Resolves into 6 canonical categories: CV, CONTRACT, MEDICAL, COURT_FILING, FINANCIAL, or OTHER.
  • Dynamic Policy Injection: Hands off domain-specific redaction guidelines from agents/policies.py to downstream detectors.

02
Hybrid Tri-Tier PII Detector
Regex + spaCy Transformers + Gemini Flash Synergy
In Simplest Words (ELI5)

Why use three different technologies instead of just asking an AI? Because relying on one tool leaves massive blind spots!

Think of catching fish in the ocean:
• Tier 1 (Regex): High-speed radar. It catches rigid patterns like 10-digit phone numbers, emails, Aadhaar cards, and PAN numbers in 3 milliseconds with 100% mathematical precision.
• Tier 2 (spaCy NLP): A grammar brain. It understands sentence syntax to spot names of people, universities, and cities, even if they aren't in any predefined dictionary.
• Tier 3 (Gemini 3.8 Flash): A human-level reader. It catches subtle, unstructured secrets like "my spouse Ananya's private phone" or "admitted to Room 304 under Dr. Mehta" that rules completely overlook!

Under The Hood (Technical Architecture)
  • Deterministic Patterns: Compiled Python re.VERBOSE expressions matching PAN ([A-Z]{5}[0-9]{4}[A-Z]), Aadhaar (\d{4}\s\d{4}\s\d{4}), emails, phone numbers, and SSNs.
  • spaCy Transformer Engine: Named Entity Recognition (NER) labeling PERSON, ORG, GPE, and DATE tokens.
  • Gemini Flash LLM: Contextual reasoning pass returning exact character span coordinates [start, end, text, entity_type].
  • Span Merge Resolver: Merges overlapping candidate intervals, resolving conflicting boundaries without creating fragmented redactions.

03
Context-Aware Verifier Agent
False-Positive Elimination & Confidence Windowing
In Simplest Words (ELI5)

Have you ever seen an AI make a silly mistake—like redacting the word "Apple" in "an apple a day keeps the doctor away", thinking Apple is a confidential company? Or redacting "Lincoln" in "Lincoln Highway" thinking it's a person's private name?

This is called a False Positive, and it ruins your document! The Verifier Agent acts as a senior editor. For every single flagged word, it looks at the 100 characters before and after it. It asks: "In this specific sentence, is this genuinely private personal information that must be hidden, or is it harmless public vocabulary?" If it's a false alarm, it discards it, keeping your document readable!

Under The Hood (Technical Architecture)
  • Symmetric Window Slicing: Extracts text[max(0, start - 100) : min(len(text), end + 100)] around candidate spans.
  • Semantic Role Evaluation: Prompts Gemini 3.8 Flash to evaluate grammatical role, public vs private entity status, and semantic intent.
  • Confidence Scoring: Computes a confidence metric (0.0 to 1.0) and rationale.
  • Threshold Filtering: Candidates scoring below 0.70 confidence or flagged as public non-PII terms are discarded immediately.

04
Structure-Preserving Redactor Engine
In-Place OpenXML Run Splicing & Style Inheritance
In Simplest Words (ELI5)

When you give a Word document (`.docx`) to most standard software, they do something awful: they dump all the text into plain text, redact the words, and create a brand new blank document.

The result? Your tables collapse, headers and corporate logos disappear, margins get messed up, and your professional typography is completely destroyed! Our Redactor Engine performs keyhole surgery. A Word file is actually a zipped package of XML files. This agent opens the internal anatomy of the document, walks into each paragraph and table cell, cuts out only the sensitive word, replaces it with [REDACTED_NAME], and clones the exact font, size, bold/italic properties, and table borders without touching anything else!

Under The Hood (Technical Architecture)
  • OpenXML Tree Navigation: Parses WordprocessingML nodes using python-docx and lxml (traversing <w:p>, <w:r>, <w:t>).
  • Run Property Deep Cloning: Clones the <w:rPr> XML node (font family, font size, bold, italic, color, kerning, underline) so the replacement mask matches the native design.
  • Multi-Run Span Splicing: Handles entities split across multiple runs by stitching text boundaries and replacing intermediate tokens.
  • Nested Scope Traversal: Recursively processes headers, footers, body paragraphs, and nested table grid cells.

05
Adversarial QA Agent
Zero-Knowledge Red Teaming & Compliance Audit Scorecard
In Simplest Words (ELI5)

In an airport, the pilot doesn't do their own security check—an independent inspector does!

The Adversarial QA Agent acts like an independent cybersecurity auditor or white-hat hacker. It is given the final redacted document with zero prior knowledge of what earlier agents did. It performs an exhaustive scan looking for any leaked phone numbers, forgotten initials, or overlooked home addresses that slipped through the cracks. It calculates a compliance score (0-100) and certifies that the document is truly safe for public distribution!

Under The Hood (Technical Architecture)
  • Black-Box Audit: Receives only the post-redaction text stream without accessing the detector's intermediate state or cache.
  • Adversarial Prompting: Instructs Gemini 3.8 Flash to hunt for partial leaks, obfuscated phone numbers (e.g. spelled out digits), and dangling personal identifiers.
  • Compliance Metric Calculation: Computes compliance_score (0-100), counts surviving risks, and assigns a verdict: approved, flagged_for_review, or rejected.
  • Audit Trail Persistence: Synchronizes the full audit report with timestamp and scorecard into MongoDB Atlas for enterprise regulatory verification.
